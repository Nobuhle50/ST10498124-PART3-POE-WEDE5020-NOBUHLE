About Hlehle's Orphanage

Our Story & Mission

Hlehle's Orphanage was established to care for children who have no parents or whose parents are unable to care for them. We exist so that no child is abandoned, left on the street, or subjected to unsafe situations.

We strongly discourage abortion and abandonment. Instead, we offer a loving, structured environment where children can grow safely until they are ready for independent life or a permanent family through adoption.

What We Provide

  • Safe accommodation and 24/7 care
  • Nutritious meals prepared by our kitchen team
  • Daily nursing care + monthly mobile clinic visits
  • Education support and school materials
  • Emotional support, counselling and life-skills training
  • Recreational activities and talent development

Our Approach to Care

We do not treat the orphanage as a place to “dump” children. Every child is treated with dignity. When a child turns 18 we support them with guidance so they leave prepared, not abandoned again. For adoptions we follow strict processes and continue post-adoption check-ins to protect children from abuse or trafficking.
