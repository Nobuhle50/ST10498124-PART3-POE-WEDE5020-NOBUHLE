Welcome to Hlehle's Orphanage

Hlehle's Orphanage is a non-profit organisation dedicated to caring for children who have lost their parents or been abandoned. We believe every child deserves a family, education, and the chance to grow up with love and dignity.

Our first priority is covering basic needs: nutritious meals, clothing, safe housing, medical care and education.

Covering Basic Needs

Daily meals, clothing, hygiene products, safe shelter, medical care and school materials.

Education & Development

School fees, books, skills training, counselling, sports and talent development.

Long-term Support

We prepare children for life after 18 through life skills, mentorship and responsible release or adoption support.
